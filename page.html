Shared Mailbox Options Comparison

Technical comparison and analysis of mechanisms to share an email address in Google Workspace, covering operational workflows, security, licensing, and manager recommendations.

1. Scope & Problem Statement

Unlike Microsoft Exchange or Microsoft 365, Google Workspace does not offer a native, free "Shared Mailbox" feature that functions identically to a normal personal mailbox across all devices.

Organizations often require shared communication addresses (e.g., contact@, accounting@, info@, support@) where multiple users must read incoming emails, avoid duplicate replies, track resolution status, and send replies from the collective identity.

Key Architectural Constraint

Sharing an email address in Google Workspace always involves an architectural compromise: either an additional Google Workspace license fee, interface limitations (web-only vs mobile app), or loss of centralized read/unread status across team members.

2. Evaluated Approaches

Four main approaches are available natively within Google Workspace to address shared email needs:

A. Google Groups (Collaborative Inbox)

A mailing list group configured as a Collaborative Inbox. Free (no extra license required). Members access conversations via groups.google.com or receive emails in their personal inbox. Supports assigning topics, tagging, and marking as resolved.

B. Gmail Account Delegation

A standard licensed Google Workspace account (e.g., team@domain.org) whose access is delegated to team members via Gmail Delegation. Provides a genuine shared Gmail UI with centralized read/unread status, shared folders, and shared sent items.

C. Email Alias / Routing Rules

An alias attached to an existing primary user or an email routing rule distributing copies to multiple recipients. Inexpensive, but lacks centralized tracking, reply visibility, and coordinated status across team members.

D. Shared User Account (Anti-Pattern)

A single licensed account where the username and password are shared among several people. Strongly discouraged: violates security policies, breaks 2FA enforcement, triggers automated lockouts/suspensions, and removes individual accountability.

4. Synthèse managériale (Recommandations & Décision)

Cette section résume les recommandations stratégiques et opérationnelles à destination des responsables d'équipes et de la direction informatique.

Option recommandée n°1 : Délégation Gmail

Pour qui ? Les services opérationnels (Comptabilité, Secrétariat, Support client, Inscriptions) où le volume d'échanges exige de savoir qui a traité ou répondu à chaque message.

Arbitrage : Nécessite le coût d'une licence Google Workspace dédiée, mais offre l'expérience utilisateur Gmail la plus propre, sans risque de double traitement des demandes.

Option recommandée n°2 : Groupe Google Collaboratif

Pour qui ? Les commissions, équipes projets temporaires ou adresses d'information générale à faible criticité.

Arbitrage : Aucun coût de licence supplémentaire. Demande cependant aux collaborateurs d'adopter le réflexe de consulter groups.google.com pour affecter et marquer les messages.

Directive de gouvernance et sécurité

Le partage d'identifiants (mot de passe commun sur un compte utilisateur) est formellement proscrit. Il est incompatible avec l'authentification multifacteur (2FA), expose l'organisation à des blocages de sécurité inopinés par Google en cas de connexions simultanées, et rend impossible toute traçabilité en cas d'erreur ou d'incident de sécurité.

5. Next Steps & Methodology

To prepare this study for full organizational adoption, the following implementation steps are outlined:

Phase 1
Inventory & Needs Assessment

Map existing shared email addresses across departments, identify participant volumes, and determine whether centralized read status or mobile access is mandatory.

Phase 2
Pilot Deployments

Conduct pilot tests for both Google Groups Collaborative Inbox and Gmail Account Delegation with representative teams.

Phase 3
Runbooks & Delegation Automation

Establish standard administrative procedures using GAM / Google Admin Console for provisioning mailboxes and managing delegate access.
