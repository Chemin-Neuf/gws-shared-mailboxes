GWS Shared Mailboxes

A comprehensive study of the 7 native mechanisms for sharing an email address in Google Workspace for Nonprofits — compared from the user's seat (familiarity, devices, daily workflow) and the administrator's seat (web console control, API automation, license cost).

📅 Last updated: 2026-09-08 ✍️ Chemin Neuf Community · Gemini 3.8 Flash · Kimi K3 🏷️ Google Workspace for Nonprofits

Overview

In Google Workspace, there is no direct 1:1 equivalent of a Microsoft Exchange "Shared Mailbox". Administrators and teams must choose among seven built-in mechanisms: a shared password account (anti-pattern), Gmail delegation, Collaborative Inbox, distribution lists, email aliases / routing, "Send Mail As" identities, and email-to-Chat Spaces — each with its own cost, usability, and governance profile.

The study is organized as 10 evaluation topics (read access, reply dynamics, organization, collaboration, history, identity, contacts, security, admin controls, API), each validated independently before verdicts are consolidated. We stay well below the tenant's 2,000-user hard limit, but license-consuming options are scrutinized accordingly. An executive decision framework (Synthèse managériale) in French summarizes the findings for leadership and department heads.

Highlights

Collaborative Inbox

No license cost. Native assignment and resolved status, but web-only and unable to compose new external mail.

Gmail Delegation

1 license per functional mailbox. Fully synced read/sent state and shared contacts for delegates; no desktop-client access.
