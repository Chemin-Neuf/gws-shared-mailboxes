Topic 4 — Team Collaboration & Status Visibility

Can the team see, at a glance, what has been read, who is handling what, and what is resolved — without asking each other? This is the core "shared inbox" promise.

Comparison of team collaboration and status visibility across the 7 options

Four collaboration signals matter operationally: shared read state, assignment to a person, resolved marking, and protection against duplicate replies. Shared reply visibility — who can see what was answered — is covered in Topic 2 — Reply & Writing Dynamics.

The two real "shared inbox" philosophies

Only two options deliver genuine, structured team coordination, with opposite philosophies: Collaborative Inbox tracks work items (assign, resolve) but ignores read state; Delegation shares the mailbox state (read, sent) but has no concept of ownership. Teams handling support-style queues prefer the former; teams sharing a correspondence identity prefer the latter.

User perspective

  • "Who is on this?" Only the Collaborative Inbox answers this natively. Everywhere else, coordination happens out-of-band (chat, meetings, shouting across the office).
  • Silent failure mode of distribution lists: either everyone replies (duplicate answers to the sender) or everyone assumes someone else replied (no answer at all). Both damage credibility.
  • Chat Space nuance: excellent for discussing a mail before answering, but the discussion and the reply live in two different tools.

Administrator perspective

Web console

Collaboration features need almost no admin configuration: Collaborative Inbox assignment/status is enabled by choosing the "Collaborative Inbox" group type; delegation has no assignment settings at all. The admin's role is mostly choosing the right architecture up front — collaboration behavior cannot be retrofitted.

API & automation

No API exposes assignment or resolved status for Groups — these live only in the Groups web UI. This is a hard limit for automation: dashboards or SLA tracking over a Collaborative Inbox are not scriptable. Details in Topic 10 — API & Automation.
